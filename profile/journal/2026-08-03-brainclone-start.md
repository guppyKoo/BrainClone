# 2026-08-03 — Starting BrainClone

Today I started BrainClone, a personal knowledge base that "clones my brain."

Purpose: keep structured documents about me (personality, values, interests, dev style, career)
so AI can represent or assist me without being re-briefed from scratch every time.

Structure: 4 areas — profile / interest / developer / career.
Management rules (OKF) are defined in CLAUDE.md; global triggers are wired in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`.

The initial drafts were reverse-engineered by AI from its work memory (Bulk Mail Electron migration,
INOS, edu-core collab-editing debugging). Next step: fill the gaps marked as inferred and confirm them.
